Horizontal cellular scaling

RAPP Roadside adds bounded on-device cells and shards RAPP Pit Crew queues by verified issue signature. It reports measured backpressure and exchanges only verified signatures, Roadside Frames, and aggregate evidence. It uses no global raw-data store, no global lock, and makes no infinity claim.

Novel verified confirmations emit inert records for the autonomous Data Bakery. Duplicates aggregate evidence without re-mining. No automatic network submission occurs.
